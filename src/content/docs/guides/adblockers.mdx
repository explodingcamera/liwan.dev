---
title: Ad Blockers
---

Some visitors use content blockers that block analytics scripts by pattern, domain name, or request path. As a result, reported traffic may be lower than actual traffic.

To reduce name-based blocking:

- **Use a neutral subdomain**: Names like `analytics.example.com` are more likely to match blocklists. A neutral name such as `a.example.com` or `liwan.example.com` may be less likely to be blocked.
- **Use a first-party domain**: Host liwan under a subdomain of your site so the tracker uses a domain you control.
